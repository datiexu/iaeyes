PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 27, 2026

IAEyes works primarily on your device and does not require an account. We do not automatically receive your break records through the app. There is no advertising or analytics tracking. In versions offering iCloud settings, journey and history sync are separate choices, both off by default. Enabling one sends that category to Apple’s private iCloud storage; it does not send it to a developer-operated server.

Version scope: TestFlight 1.0.0 build 18 stores app records locally and has no iCloud sync controls. The optional sync terms below apply only to later versions that display the iCloud controls; a website update does not activate syncing in older versions.

1. Who operates IAEyes and what this policy covers

IAEyes is provided and operated by independent developer Datie Xu (the “developer,” “we” or “us”), who is also responsible for processing the personal information described in this policy. This policy covers our local Apple-platform versions for iPhone, iPad and Mac, this website and our support process. Availability on each device depends on the versions actually released in the relevant store. Privacy contact: itcrown2025@gmail.com.

The app offers breaks during work and image simulations. Image choices and personal notes are not health measurements or medical conclusions.

Visual and usage preferencesFor example, interface language, flip pace, reduced motion settings and whether the journey is on. These restore your chosen experience and are stored locally.
Journey progressThe selected level, highest unlocked level, accumulated active time at that level and final completion state. Stored locally; the earned-progress snapshot can optionally sync as described below. The selected replay level and personal display calibration do not sync.

This local content is not automatically sent to us. The app does not ask for your name, phone number, prescription or eye health information. Apart from the optional distance reminder and the clarity check described above, it does not use the camera; it does not use the microphone, location, contacts or HealthKit data. You can turn the distance reminder off in Adjust at any time, or withdraw camera permission in system settings. Current versions include no advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs.

Medal images (versions offering this feature): medals are derived on your device from earned journey progress and passed challenges. Saving or sharing creates a PNG containing only the medal, level, chapter and IAEyes text, without dates, scores, feelings or detailed records. On iPhone/iPad, saving asks only for permission to add photos; the app does not read your photo library. Mac saving uses the location you select. Sharing opens the system menu and sends the image only to a destination you choose. Photos, selected folders or receiving services may sync or process your exported copy under their own settings and terms. Deleting app or cloud records does not erase exported copies; remove those in the destination app or folder. Temporary app export files are removed when the operation finishes.

3. Optional iCloud sync, retention, deletion and backups

Your choice: journey sync and detailed history sync are independently off on each device until you enable them in the app’s iCloud panel. Enabling a category authorizes its existing local data and future changes to be uploaded to your private CloudKit database, and downloaded on other devices where that category is enabled under the same iCloud account. No separate IAEyes account is created. Your iCloud account can differ from the App Store account used for purchases.

Data sent: journey sync sends only the journey schema version, highest unlocked level, active milliseconds at that level and final completion flag. It does not send detailed history. History sync sends the completed records described above, plus record edit revisions and a random app-install identifier used to resolve conflicts. App sync does not include clarity checks, personal starting size, camera frames, distance settings, language or timer preferences. Camera frames are never recorded, stored or uploaded, even when either sync option is on.

Purpose and provider: Apple operates iCloud/CloudKit for cross-device storage and delivery under its Privacy Policy and iCloud terms. Apple may process account, device, network and service information to deliver it; storage location and international processing depend on Apple’s service and your account settings. We do not operate an app sync server or access your private CloudKit database through a developer administration interface, and do not use this data for advertising or tracking. We do not promise end-to-end encryption beyond the protections Apple actually provides for this service.

Merging and availability: the highest earned level is retained. At the same level, the larger saved time is retained rather than adding device totals, so concurrent or offline practice may not all count. Time never carries into the next level, and syncing never substitutes for the level’s time requirement or a passed challenge. Record IDs prevent duplicates; concurrent edits to a feeling use deterministic revision rules, and you can edit it again after merging. Sync is attempted while the app is open and idle, with later retries after offline failures. It needs a functioning iCloud account, network access and available iCloud capacity; it is not a guaranteed backup. An iCloud account change or sign-out detected by the app turns both switches off and keeps local copies. Review local data before explicitly enabling sync with another account.

Turning off and deleting: turning a category off on one device stops future app sync for it there and keeps its local and iCloud copies; requests already sent may finish, and other enabled devices can continue. The app offers separate “Delete cloud progress” and “Delete cloud history” actions with confirmation. They remove that category’s cloud payload and turn it off on the requesting device, leaving local copies and a minimal deletion marker. Other devices disable that category when they next sync. Re-enabling it later is a new choice that can upload remaining local data again. Device/system backups are separate; manage their copies separately. The developer cannot remotely retrieve or erase your private iCloud content for you.

Local preferences and records do not expire automatically. They remain until you clear the relevant app data. There is no account, and the developer does not automatically receive these break records through the app, so we cannot look them up or delete them remotely.

  • On iPhone or iPad, go to Settings → General → iPhone / iPad Storage → IAEyes and choose “Delete App” to remove the app and its local data. “Offload App” keeps documents and data and is not a substitute for deletion.
  • On Mac, deleting the app itself may leave local files behind. Email support for data removal instructions that match your version and installation source. You do not need to send us your record files.
  • Device backup, restoration or migration may separately copy or restore local content, depending on the system, app version configuration and your settings. Backups are separate from the optional app iCloud sync above. System backups are managed under the relevant platform’s policies and controls. To remove backup copies, manage them separately in the system or backup service.
