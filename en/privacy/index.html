PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 23, 2026

IAEyes works primarily on your device and does not require an account. We do not automatically receive your break records through the app. The app has no advertising, analytics tracking or developer-operated cloud sync.

1. Who operates IAEyes and what this policy covers

IAEyes is provided and operated by independent developer Datie Xu (the “developer,” “we” or “us”), who is also responsible for processing the personal information described in this policy. This policy covers our local Apple-platform versions for iPhone, iPad and Mac, this website and our support process. Availability on each device depends on the versions actually released in the relevant store. Privacy contact: itcrown2025@gmail.com.

The app offers breaks during work and image simulations. Image choices and personal notes are not health measurements or medical conclusions.

2. What the app stores on your device

InformationPurpose and storage
Visual and usage preferencesFor example, interface language, flip pace, reduced motion settings and whether the journey is on. These restore your chosen experience and are stored locally.
Journey progressThe level you are on, so the next break continues from it. Stored locally.
Completed break recordsFor example, activity identifiers, dates, categories, actual timed duration, settings, flip counts, the level played, how many end-of-break challenge questions you got right and whether you passed, and optional notes about how it felt. These are stored locally to display your history.
Camera frames (optional distance reminder)Only after you turn on the distance reminder, and only while a break runs, the front camera is used to estimate your distance from the screen on the device and pause the break when you are too close. Frames are processed in memory one at a time and are never recorded, stored or uploaded; the camera turns off when the break ends. Only the on/off setting is saved.
Camera frames (clarity check)Only when you start the near-and-far part of the clarity check, the front camera estimates your distance in the same way: nothing recorded, stored or uploaded, and the camera turns off when that part ends.
Clarity check resultsFor each check: the date, screen type and size, the rough distance range that looked sharp, and your answers to the red/green and lines questions. Stored locally to compare with the next check. They describe what you saw on the screen; they are not vision or health data.
Timed break settingsYour work interval, break length and next reminder time, stored locally. On mobile, reminders are local system notifications and need your notification permission; they are scheduled on your device and do not pass through the developer.

This local content is not automatically sent to us. The app does not ask for your name, phone number, prescription or eye health information. Apart from the optional distance reminder and the clarity check described above, it does not use the camera; it does not use the microphone, location, contacts or HealthKit data. You can turn the distance reminder off in Adjust at any time, or withdraw camera permission in system settings. Current versions include no advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs.

3. Retention, deletion and system backups

Local preferences and records do not expire automatically. They remain until you clear the relevant app data. There is no account, and the developer does not automatically receive these break records through the app, so we cannot look them up or delete them remotely.

  • On iPhone or iPad, go to Settings → General → iPhone / iPad Storage → IAEyes and choose “Delete App” to remove the app and its local data. “Offload App” keeps documents and data and is not a substitute for deletion.
  • On Mac, deleting the app itself may leave local files behind. Email support for data removal instructions that match your version and installation source. You do not need to send us your record files.
  • Device backup, restoration or migration may separately copy or restore local content, depending on the system, app version configuration and your settings. The app does not provide its own cloud sync. System backups are managed under the relevant platform’s policies and controls. To remove backup copies, manage them separately in the system or backup service.

4. When you choose to contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments, device details or version information you choose to provide. We use them to answer questions, investigate issues and handle privacy requests. The email provider processes messages under its own terms. Please avoid sending identity documents, medical records, eye photos or other unnecessary sensitive information.

We retain support information only while needed to handle the request and keep necessary follow-up records, and delete it when no longer needed. You may use the email above to request access to, correction of or deletion of support information you have submitted to us, except information we must retain by law.

If you choose to submit an issue on GitHub, its contents may be public and are subject to GitHub’s rules. Please use email for privacy matters.

5. Third-party websites and this website

External reference materials in the app and third-party links on the support page are provided by their respective websites. You visit a third-party website only when you open its link. Please review that provider’s privacy policy.

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It embeds no advertising, visitor analytics scripts, third-party fonts or tracking pixels. GitHub may process IP addresses and request logs to provide hosting and maintain security; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. Visiting this website does not automatically send your local app records to GitHub.

6. Security, minors and policy updates

The security of local data also depends on your device lock, system permissions and backup settings. We process support information only through channels you choose to use. We do not sell local break records or support information, or use them for advertising profiles.

We do not seek to collect children’s personal information. Minors may use the app with a guardian’s guidance. If you believe a child has submitted unnecessary personal information through support, contact the email above so we can address it.

Policy updates will be dated on this page. If the purposes of processing, upload behavior or third-party services change materially, we will update the explanation before enabling the relevant feature and seek your consent where applicable.

Current versions have no ads. If advertising or sponsored content is introduced in the future, we will identify the actual providers, information involved and purposes before enabling it, update this policy and the store privacy disclosures, and obtain required permissions and provide controls under applicable law and platform rules. This policy does not constitute advance consent to future data collection or advertising tracking.

7. Contact

For questions about this policy, local data removal or support information, email itcrown2025@gmail.com or visit Help & Support.
