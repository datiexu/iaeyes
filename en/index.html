A LITTLE PAUSE, JUST FOR YOU

Love your eyes.
Give them a little break.

Let work wait a moment. Watch the lens images change, or look away from the screen at something far away.

Help & contactYour privacy

A break utility by independent developer Datie Xu. The first App Store versions for iPhone, iPad and Mac are in preparation.

01 / A CHANGE OF SCENE

Choose a lens effect

Concave and convex lens illustrations, with flips, zooming and changing images. Choose a pace that feels comfortable.

02 / A MOMENT AWAY

Give the view 20 seconds

Put the screen aside and look at something genuinely far away. There is no rush, and you can stop whenever you like.

03 / YOUR BREAKS

Look back at your breaks

The duration of your lens breaks and distance breaks, along with how they felt, stays on your device.

The lenses and virtual diopter values are screen image effects. They do not change your actual viewing distance. Image choices and personal notes describe your break experience; they are not health measurements or medical assessments.
