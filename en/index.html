A LITTLE PAUSE, JUST FOR YOU

Let your eyes
roll.

Been working a while? Give your eyes a minute: convex and concave lenses flip and travel the whole screen, and after 20 minutes at a level a short challenge moves you up, level by level.

Help & contactYour privacy

A break utility by independent developer Datie Xu. iPhone, iPad and Mac versions are in TestFlight testing and not yet on the App Store.

01 / 100 LEVELS

A new change every 10 levels

Start with a sphere lens and one panel, then add flip directions, letters, light and shade, colour pairs, prisms, two panels… The picture gets a little smaller every level, and nothing goes back.

02 / EYE PATHS

Across the whole screen

Side to side, up and down, circles, figure eights, a star, a zigzag… Each level has its own path, followed in full screen. Reduced motion keeps things still.

03 / A SHORT CHALLENGE

Pass it, move up

Once a level has 20 minutes of breaks, a break ends with the challenge: name the direction or letter five times; four right passes, then move up or stay. Records and how it felt stay on your device.

The lenses and virtual diopter values are screen image effects. They do not change your actual viewing distance. IAEyes is not a medical product and does not diagnose, treat or improve any eye condition; levels, challenge results and notes describe your break experience and are not health measurements.
