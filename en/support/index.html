Frequently asked questions

What does IAEyes do?

IAEyes is a small utility for breaks during work. Watch the flipping lens images change, or spend 20 seconds looking away at something genuinely far away. Break duration and personal notes can be saved on your device.

Do the ±D values represent real lens prescriptions?

They are virtual image settings that change lens appearance and visual effects, not prescription values. Screen zooming, concave and convex appearances, and changes in clarity do not change your actual viewing distance or equal the optical effects of physical lenses.

Too much movement, or need a pause?

You can pause or stop, choose a gentler pace and smaller changes, or enable reduced motion. Choose what feels comfortable without aiming for a number of flips. Stop if you feel uncomfortable.

Are records uploaded? Do I need to log in?

Current local Apple-platform versions need no account. Preferences, completed records and optional notes stay on your device and are not automatically uploaded to the developer. There is no developer-operated cloud sync. Your device’s backup or migration settings may affect saved copies; see the Privacy Policy.

How do I clear data on iPhone or iPad?

Go to Settings → General → iPhone / iPad Storage → IAEyes and choose “Delete App.” “Offload App” keeps documents and data. If a system backup still contains older copies, manage those separately in backup settings.

Does deleting the Mac app also delete its data?

Not necessarily. Deleting the app itself may leave local files behind. Email us with your app version and installation source for the appropriate removal steps. You do not need to send your record files.

Do before-and-after image choices show a health change?

The choices describe only the images you preferred and your sense of ease during that break. They cannot confirm physiological relaxation, changes in vision or therapeutic effects. Features depend on what your version actually offers.
